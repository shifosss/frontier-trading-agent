# One Person Trading Agent

An autonomous quantitative research laboratory with a verifiable portfolio operator.

**Status:** architecture and delivery plan under review. No trading system, backtest result, deployed service, or profitable strategy is claimed yet.

The project aims to make one person capable of running a disciplined research and trading operation: acquire evidence, generate hypotheses, execute reproducible experiments, compare candidates, operate frozen strategies, and investigate the difference between expected and observed behavior.

- [System design and decision records](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-18-frontier-trading-system-design.md)
- [Delivery roadmap and acceptance criteria](docs/roadmap.md)
- [Design review and resolved objections](docs/design-review.md)

The confirmed first proving ground is US and Canadian stocks and ETFs, with holding periods from hours to weeks. USD/CAD accounting, both countries' calendars, and market-specific data are first-class requirements. Use existing access and free resources; ask before new spending. Research and paper operation are the initial modes.

The distinctive capabilities are experiment lineage, point-in-time evidence, autonomous research with a measured budget, prospective strategy comparisons, decision replay, and execution attribution. A claim of state-of-the-art performance requires a named benchmark, fixed comparison budget, reproducible evaluation, and results.
