# Fixed ETF residual-reversal comparison

This asks whether unusually negative two-session ETF returns, after removing their recent sensitivity to SPY, recover during a short cash-funded holding period. It adds diversified equity instruments and a fitted relative-price mechanism to the existing research. It does not assume that an unexplained return is a temporary liquidity shock: sector news and changing factor exposure can also produce residuals.

The methodological precedent is residual-based contrarian trading, but the published evidence is for different portfolios. Avellaneda and Lee model individual-stock residuals against ETFs or statistical factors in long/short portfolios; their results do not establish a profitable long-only ETF strategy. [Original author paper](https://math.nyu.edu/~avellane/AvellanedaLeeStatArb20090616.pdf). Da, Liu and Schaumburg distinguish reversals associated with liquidity from fundamental information; this motivates separating common-market movement, but our price-only model does not identify fundamental news. [Original author paper](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/Reversal.pdf).

The exact formula is frozen in definition.json. Sixty fitting returns give a modest sample for two OLS parameters, while ending the fit before the two formation returns prevents the current shock from immediately changing its own reference model. The -2 threshold is one conventional dislocation rule, not a statistical significance claim. Its denominator assumes two daily residual variances add; autocorrelation and parameter uncertainty remain limitations. Near-perfect floating-point fits are marked unavailable with one fixed roundoff rule rather than a variance floor that could create very large artificial scores.

The trend arm is a separate hypothesis: residual weakness during a persistent aggregate decline may reflect continuing deleveraging rather than a temporary dislocation. The existing 200-session SPY trend definition supplies a predetermined, slow condition. It can also miss sharp recoveries. Both arms will be reported, with no later selection of the more favorable market gate.

The control uses exactly the unfiltered reversal's decision dates and candidate count, then a fixed hash ordering within the same causally eligible ten-ETF pool. This avoids permanent alphabetical preference without a random-seed search. Overlap with reversal candidates is allowed. Held-symbol exclusions, whole-share prices, ATR sizing and settled cash can still produce different executed counts and exposure; the control is not a claim of exact risk or exposure matching. Five cash-engine slots can limit all arms even when all ten names signal.

The proposal's two-session hold cannot be passed to the existing unchanged engine, whose admitted horizons are 1/3/5. Before any signals or outcomes, this study chooses three sessions: still a short correction window and within the user's maximum one-week mandate. It does not create a second horizon comparison or modify the shared engine. Signals use the official close plus one minute and fill at the next close; the resulting delay may consume the edge and is part of this test, not a detail to repair after seeing losses.

All twelve accounts use the same 2017–2025 entry calendar and 2016 warmup: three arms, USD10,000/2,000, and two broker profiles. A year-end signal on 2016-12-30 can first enter on 2017-01-03. A 2026 liquidation tail prevents artificial end-of-period exit truncation. Broker friction is independently fixed after the quote probe and before any account results. Larger capital can reduce whole-share/fixed-fee drag; it cannot improve the underlying percentage signal by itself.

The reusable interface is frontier.short_horizon_study._simulate_daily(..., horizon=3, signal_ranking=..., signal_counts=..., state=..., process_through=...), followed by _summarize. The isolated adapter must retain the prior filing adapter's original fee-inclusive reservation cap and carry cash, positions and settlements continuously. It must also enforce the newly declared 30% close-mark drawdown halt without altering frozen engine code: cancel queued entries, schedule remaining positions for next-close liquidation, and preserve any gap or missing-price failure. The prior filing event_rankings function is not applicable to daily ETF signals. Existing read_history, normalized_atr convention and build_settlement_indices provide the source/ATR/settlement pieces.

Focused checks should establish OLS indexing and an independent numeric reference, formation-window exclusion, future-price invariance, zero/near-perfect residual availability, exact deterministic control counts, gate causality, and missing-price retention. Account-adapter checks must establish next-close timing, three-session independent exits, original reservations, historical settlement, permanent drawdown halt/overshoot, and continuous state. These are meaningful timing and accounting checks, not a new framework.

The source sample consists of ten currently listed ETFs plus SPY from the already frozen fourteen-ETF inventory. Prices and distribution adjustments are retrospective, and the existing engine values nominal entry notional using adjusted-return ratios. This is a synthetic total-return convention, not reconstructed dividend receivables, split entitlements or tax accounting. All sources, action records, omissions and invalid paths remain visible. Results will be exploratory and cannot establish a weekly return floor or authorize live trading.
