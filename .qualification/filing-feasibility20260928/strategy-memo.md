# One fundamental experiment: five-session post-filing income surprise

Research proposal dated 2026-09-28. **No event-strategy backtest, fitted model, trading result or new bulk source capture is reported here.** Freeze a separate source/experiment definition before execution. The intraday study and original daily results remain independent evidence.

Prefer a bounded test of **unusually positive seasonal GAAP income change after a quarterly filing becomes usable**. It adds issuer financial information missing from the existing price/volume and conditional models. Use the exact `NetIncomeLoss` concept and assets instead of assuming historical EPS/share units have been reconciled. This is our short-horizon post-filing hypothesis, not a replication of analyst-consensus earnings surprise or a claim that the literature proves five-session profitability.

The economic reason to add information is concrete. All 18 conditional accounts lost under their declared costs. Calibration attenuation improved survival, but its only positive account earned USD10.05 on three trades; two Moomoo scenarios stayed entirely in cash. The other three active shrinkage paths lost 5.67%, 8.64% and 32.35%. These observations motivate testing a different information source, rather than manufacturing larger return forecasts or relaxing admissions. They do not require a simple strategy to become profitable before richer models may be tested. See the completed local conditional and shrinkage economic results.

## What the research supports

| Candidate | Distinct information | Evidence and practical limit |
|---|---|---|
| Seasonal unexpected earnings | Change in issuer profitability relative to its own prior-year quarter and historical variation | Livnat and Mendenhall find stronger drift for analyst-based surprises than time-series forecasts; those surprise definitions are not interchangeable. The proposed income/assets measure below is a deliberately different proxy. Its five-session usefulness remains to be tested. |
| Filing textual changes | New uncertainty, demand, cost or business-condition language relative to previous disclosure | Feldman et al. find incremental information in MD&A tone changes beyond earnings/accruals. Their principal drift interval starts two days after filing and runs through the day after the next quarter's preliminary earnings announcement. That is not evidence for a one-week holding rule. Original sections, parsing quality and time-bounded language representations still need qualification. |
| Earnings-release versus post-filing drift | The first public release may contain the actual information shock, while a later filing adds detail | An earnings exhibit can arrive before a 10-Q. An SEC acceptance timestamp cannot retroactively make later 10-Q facts available at the earlier announcement. A post-filing test is slower but can have a reproducible accession-level input set. Its reduced remaining edge is part of what must be measured. |

Primary studies: [Livnat and Mendenhall, 2006](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1475-679X.2006.00196.x); [Feldman et al., published paper hosted by the author's university](https://bschool-en.huji.ac.il/sites/default/files/businesshe/files/managements_tone_change_post_earnings_announcement_drift_and_accruals_0.pdf). The comparison above does not infer a profitable stock strategy from a different holding period.

## Exact proposed signal and information boundaries

Begin with the same six named operating-company pilot stocks: AAPL, MSFT, F, T, HRL and BANF, with SPY as a descriptive market-return reference. Their inclusion precedes this event study's outcomes. Use first, non-amended 10-Q filings for fiscal Q1/Q2/Q3 during 2017–2025, with 2012–2016 filings solely for history. Retain every requested issuer/filing and each failure. Do not add replacement names, select profitable issuers, derive Q4 from a later annual report, or substitute a later amendment when the original is unavailable. This modest current-member pilot is a source/economic feasibility study, not the final broad universe or a survivorship-corrected sample.

For each filing event e, extract from the **same original accession**:

- Current-quarter `us-gaap:NetIncomeLoss`, its corresponding prior-year quarter comparator, and `us-gaap:Assets` at the prior fiscal year-end. Require consistent entity-wide, non-segment contexts and USD units, with original XBRL scale/sign/context retained. Do not substitute `ProfitLoss`: where noncontrolling interests are distinguished, the concepts can represent different amounts. Verify genuine three-month fiscal periods from the statements, including 52/53-week calendars; do not confuse current-quarter and year-to-date durations.
- `u[e] = (NI_current_quarter - NI_prior_year_comparator) / Assets_prior_fiscal_year_end`.
- `S[e] = u[e] / sample_sd(previous eight u values)`, using the issuer's immediately preceding eight scheduled comparable 10-Q events. All eight must already be publicly usable and have qualified inputs; a missing event interrupts the history. Zero variance means unavailable, not an arbitrary variance floor. No winsorization, inferred missing values or cross-issuer borrowing.

The treatment buys only `S>=1`, ranking simultaneous candidates by descending S with alphabetical ties. One historical standard deviation is a fixed unusual-change threshold, not a predicted return or a fitted confidence level. No sign reversal, threshold sweep, alternate scaler or horizon grid is added. The zero-return source of seasonal expectation is the comparable prior-year income; scaling by assets reduces size/unit differences without using per-share split adjustments. Issuance, acquisition, tax effects and one-off GAAP gains can still distort economic comparability; record such explanations without excluding inconvenient outcomes after returns are known.

Current-filing comparative numbers are allowed because they are disclosed by the decision time. They may reflect accounting changes already reported in that filing. Each *previous* u remains the value reconstructed from its own original accession; a later restatement must not overwrite the history the earlier strategy would have known. Companyfacts is an accession-tagged locator/cross-check, not permission to use the latest value for an earlier period. SEC frames select a recently filed fact for a calendar period and therefore are not a point-in-time feature table. [SEC API contract](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces).

No price-return label trains this first signal. The eight-event scaler is computed afresh from earlier disclosed inputs only. Report every calendar year and the predeclared 2017–2021 versus 2022–2025 segments; the latter is a chronology check, not an untouched market-data claim, because these price periods have already been researched elsewhere.

## A clock that can actually be implemented

Retain issuer CIK, accession, form, public filing date, raw acceptance timestamp, its verified timezone interpretation, fiscal period, document paths, source hashes and retrieval vintage. Use the complete submission/index together with the submissions metadata; inconsistent timestamps or identities remain unavailable. Do not infer UTC solely from an unverified parser representation.

Let D be the later of the verified acceptance date in America/New_York and the public filing date. The conservative information floor is **end of D in New York plus one hour**. Plan at the next regular session's 09:00 ET only if that floor has passed; otherwise defer to the following session. Enter at that session's close, using a previously completed market-data session for sizing; exit at the close five exchange sessions later. This is a full overnight processing convention, not instantaneous acceptance-time execution. For an exceptional early-close session, the same 09:00 decision precedes its actual close.

This delay matters: SEC guidance says many submissions beginning after 17:30 ET receive the following business day's filing date and are disseminated then. The maximum-date rule avoids treating those as tradable the preceding evening. Earlier press-release/8-K times should be recorded to report release-to-filing lag when verified, but they do not move this strategy's entry earlier. A later event implementation must preserve these timestamps rather than relabel a post-close filing as information known at the preceding close. [SEC filing-status guidance](https://www.sec.gov/submit-filings/filer-support-resources/how-do-i-guides/determine-status-my-filing); [SEC dissemination/index guidance](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data).

## The small comparison and actual account hurdle

Declare **four continuous cash accounts**, all with a fixed five-session hold:

1. The seasonal-income treatment above, under each of the two existing cost scenarios.
2. A filing-event control using the identical structural/data eligibility and eight-event history, but omitting the S threshold; simultaneous candidates rank alphabetically. It buys the same kind of filing opportunity without selecting a positive innovation.

Both use USD2,000, whole shares, at most five independent positions, the existing 20%-NAV entry cap and 0.5%-NAV risk sizing with known two-ATR distance, and fixed settled-cash reservations including entry fees. Quantity may decrease for fill affordability, never increase from later information. Skip an already held issuer; an unfilled event expires at its declared entry rather than being retried until its outcome looks favorable. Preserve historical T+3/T+2/T+1 settlement and original corporate-action/source limitations. Missing required entry/exit prices invalidate that account path; future endpoint availability cannot screen earlier events. No real account, paper migration or new subscription is involved.

Costs stay `proportional` 40-bps and `moomoo_ca_us` plus 40-bps, exactly as already declared. At USD400 notional and a USD10 reference price, the frozen Moomoo formula totals about USD5.59 for an unchanged-price round trip, or 1.40%; at USD200 it is about 2.40%. This includes the scenario's regulatory minimum, but not funding FX, account costs or omitted charges. These are counterfactual cost arithmetic, not a verified historical tariff or measured fills. Research also finds that transaction costs materially reduce implementable earnings-drift profits, especially where the apparent anomaly is strongest. [Ng, Rusticus and Verdi, 2008](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1475-679X.2008.00290.x). Low event frequency saves turnover but does not create a return edge.

In addition to the four funded paths, preserve all structurally eligible five-session stock and SPY returns, missing endpoints, and the S-selected/unselected event groups. Report equal-date descriptive event means and within-issuer counts; these groups occur on different dates and are not randomized causal controls. The funded comparison must show exposure and fees so that keeping cash cannot be mistaken for superior return prediction.

## Decision and failure criteria

Source feasibility comes first: do the actual retained original accessions supply the clock, same-context facts and prior history? An unresolved access/unit/identity/timestamp issue is unavailable evidence, not a reason to backfill future data. A source-feasible but unprofitable result is a valid completed experiment.

Report all four accounts, trade expectancy, net dollars, fees, weekly/monthly/yearly returns, drawdowns, exposure, funding constraints, release-to-filing lag, data failures and stock/trade concentration. Reject the proposal as a usable allocation rule in this pilot if the selected events show no positive delayed market-relative association, if funded net expectancy remains nonpositive under the relevant fee route, or if treatment improvement is only lower exposure while active trades lose. A path exceeding the owner's 30% drawdown limit is not operationally acceptable. If the selected sample has fewer than 30 matched events, four issuers or five calendar years, call it insufficiently supported; do not lower S to obtain activity. A few trades cannot establish robust profitability, as the completed shrinkage comparison already illustrates.

A positive result would justify broader *source-qualified, return-independent* issuer sampling, not automatic promotion. A negative six-name pilot would not prove universal absence of the effect. Filing text can be tested as a separately declared incremental information source even if this simple income control loses; the prerequisite is reliable input chronology, not baseline profitability. A fixed historical dictionary or representations trained only on earlier filings could support that comparison. Asking a modern LLM to forget subsequent company history does not remove pretraining contamination. Contemporary LLMs may help audit exact extracted source spans, but their uncited remembered issuer facts or forecasts must not enter historical features. [Glasserman and Lin, look-ahead/distraction analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17322).
