Wednesday, December 24, 2025
Equity Corporate Actions Alert #2025 - 708
Information Regarding the Reverse Stock Split, Par Value, and CUSIP Number Change for WORK Medical Technology Group LTD (WOK)
Category:
Industry Announcement
Markets Impacted:
The Nasdaq Stock Market
Contact Information:
Nasdaq Corporate Data Operations at +1 877 308 0523
Nasdaq Trading Services at +1 212 231 5100
Resources
Press Release
WORK Medical Technology Group LTD (WOK) will effect a one-for-one hundred (1-100) reverse split and par value change of its Class A Ordinary Shares. The reverse stock split and par value change from US$0.05 to US$5.00 will become effective on Monday, December 29, 2025. In conjunction with the reverse split, the CUSIP number will change to G9767H125.
If you would like to register as a market maker in WOK, contact Nasdaq Trading Services at +1 212 231 5100.
