# Three proposed tests for the options and ETF extension

Design proposal only; no new capture, fit, strategy selection or account run. Use USD10,000 as the primary account and USD2,000 as the same-policy affordability comparison. The target is whole-account weekly performance, including idle cash, fees and losing weeks. Five to fifteen percent means USD500–1,500 or USD100–300 respectively. It is an aspiration to evaluate, not an expected return.

The saved evidence does not support scaling existing models to reach that target. All 42 saved learned/ensemble USD40bps paths were retained: none of 21 USD2,000 paths and one of 21 USD10,000 paths was positive over its entire 2003–2026 history. The latter earned 2.7846% over the full path, not per week. The highest arithmetic mean weekly return at USD10,000 was 0.010412%. Four paths per capital size had observed drawdown at or below 30%; that is not an enforced loss ceiling. This descriptive comparison does not rerun or promote them.

## 1. Cash-funded ETF liquidity reversal

**Economic question:** Do temporary broad-index or sector-relative selling dislocations reverse over the next two sessions after accounting for the common market move? This tests liquidity recovery in diversified instruments, distinct from adding another individual-stock momentum threshold.

Start with a small declared set of broad/sector ETFs whose saved histories and original symbol identities actually qualify; SPY is the initial verified reference. Build a causal residual against SPY using only prior observations, then test one predeclared oversold-residual rule with an aggregate-market stress condition. Hold two sessions, screen daily, buy whole ETF shares using settled cash and the existing independent-position ledger. No borrowing, ETF shorting, inverse or leveraged ETF substitution. Freeze eligibility, residual window and one entry rule before reading its outcomes. Compare against equal-risk ETF exposure on the same eligible decision dates and cash, including both entry timing and costs.

**Why first:** The stock/ETF historical infrastructure already exists, while this mechanism and vehicle are different from the completed failed stock grid. Additional ETF histories must be verified rather than presumed available. Passing an underlying test would not establish option profitability.

## 2. Directional long-option expression of conditional continuation

**Economic question:** Can paying a limited premium for directional convexity improve account-level utility on the strongest historically calibrated continuation signals, after time decay, volatility repricing and spreads? The option is an execution choice whose cost must earn its place, not a way to manufacture an edge through leverage.

Propose a cash-paid long SPY call for positive continuation or long SPY put for negative continuation, using an as-of historical chain, one deterministic near-the-money choice and an expiry initially proposed at 21–45 calendar days. Hold three sessions and close before expiry. Test the same underlying signal with cash-funded ETF exposure where feasible, while reporting the unavailable short-direction cash-equity control explicitly. Preserve exact underlying forecast and decision dates across comparisons; do not select contracts by later option returns, volume or survival. A bought put needs no stock short, but its whole premium can be lost.

**Present capability:** Six SPY option symbols and six daily-bar sessions are verified. This enables source plumbing only. A performance study needs historical as-of contract membership, verified deliverables/multipliers and synchronized decision/fill bid/ask observations through exit. Existing access to those fields has not been demonstrated. Use no synthetic option prices or midpoint fills to claim tradability.

## 3. Event-conditioned long volatility, without short legs

**Economic question:** Do rare, identifiable macro-event states produce a sufficiently large move or volatility repricing to justify buying both a call and put? This is a volatility exposure, economically different from ETF reversal or directional continuation.

Propose one SPY long straddle around a single predeclared family of scheduled macro releases, with the historical release schedule known before entry, one fixed next-session exit and both legs sharing a verified expiry/strike. Selection would compare a training-only conditional move distribution with the actual total premium and two-leg costs; the test must allow all-cash outcomes. The return depends on time value and implied-volatility changes as well as directionless movement, so an underlying realized-volatility proxy is only a source/forecast diagnostic, never an option P&L replacement. Freeze one event family and contract rule before observing outcomes.

**Present capability:** This is the furthest from runnable. The fixture lacks synchronized two-leg quotes, as-of chain membership and historical event clocks. Do not assign a return forecast or rank this above the other ideas before those inputs are qualified. Do not substitute short-volatility credit spreads, naked selling or expiration exercise to avoid the missing data.

## Shared account design and decision gates

Use no new spending. Continue the already declared cash-backed earnings study independently; these proposals do not change its cohort or features. Prioritize the ETF reversal test, then a bounded existing-access contract/quote feasibility check for the option arms. Any such new capture requires its own fixed source declaration; none is authorized or run by this document alone.

For all eventual tests, use the same calendar and continuous settled-cash accounts at both balances, no deposits that disguise losses, no portfolio borrowing and a maximum five-session holding period. Integer contracts and verified contract terms are mandatory. If the smallest unit breaches the predeclared premium/loss budget, stay in cash; never replace it with a cheaper farther-out-of-the-money contract after seeing outcomes. Single-leg and combined-straddle premium caps and ETF sizing must be fixed before returns are read. A proposed conservative option starting point is at most 1% of NAV in total premium per position and 3% open premium across positions, with no scale-up to chase a weekly target. This may make USD2,000 frequently infeasible; that result matters. Final numerical risk settings remain a design choice to freeze, not an optimized result.

Enforce a 30% observed portfolio-drawdown halt in the new account specification: cancel new entries and model liquidation at the next feasible execution after crossing. Report any gap/slippage overshoot; a stop rule cannot guarantee a 30% bound. Long options must be closed with verified executable liquidity before expiration; unavailable exit or contract terms leaves the path invalid and reported. Retain all declared accounts, rejected entries and data failures.

Judge net whole-account weekly distribution, frequency of weeks above 5/10/15%, losing streaks, drawdown, capital at risk, turnover, exposure, affordability rejections, costs and contribution concentration. Use training-only chronological selection/calibration and retain all outer-test results. The current capital summary cannot supply weekly target-hit frequencies, so none are inferred from its averages. Falsify each economic mechanism before adding model complexity; no threshold or fee selection to force trades or profits.
