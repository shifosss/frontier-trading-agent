# Next distinct alpha source: bounded research decision

**Research memo only, September 28, 2026. No implementation, new capture, experiment, universe change or trading result.** The current 18-account conditional study remains primary. Its saved-forecast shrinkage comparison remains a separate bounded alternative.

Prefer one small **delayed, session-aligned opening-range continuation versus reversal experiment** before building a filing strategy. This preference reflects demonstrated source access and a clean falsifiable timing question, not a claim that hourly trading is profitable. Filing-based information is the more distinct input family, but its usable historical event clock and as-reported accounting data are not yet established locally.

## What is genuinely different

| Direction | Mechanism and primary evidence | What we have; what is missing |
|---|---|---|
| Hourly opening continuation/reversal | Persistent order flow or information may continue an opening move; temporary liquidity pressure may reverse it. Heston, Korajczyk and Sadka find sub-hour reversals related to temporary liquidity imbalance and bid/ask bounce. Their same-time-of-day continuation finding is a separate effect. Gao et al. find early-to-late intraday momentum in ETFs; Baltussen et al. connect late-day momentum to hedging in futures. None directly establishes a delayed, long-only individual-stock opening-range strategy. | Existing Alpaca credentials returned raw SIP hourly AAPL/MSFT data in January 2016 and complete minute bars for one 2024 early-close session. Six-stock historical SIP quote coverage was also verified. Broad minute capture, executable delayed signals and hourly account results are **not** implemented. |
| Public earnings/filing drift | Underreaction to unexpected earnings or new disclosure content may persist after publication. A seasonal earnings forecast avoids paid analyst consensus, but it is not an equivalent replacement: Livnat and Mendenhall find stronger drift using analyst surprises than time-series surprises. Feldman et al. find incremental information in MD&A tone changes. Their principal drift window extends toward the next earnings announcement, so it does not establish a five-session edge. | SEC public submission/XBRL APIs require no keys. Existing daily price/cash infrastructure is reusable. A retained accession-level event history, verified release/acceptance clock, as-reported quarterly fundamentals, issuer mapping and historical filing-text signals are missing. No earnings lane was found among the existing strategy modules/docs inspected. |

Primary studies: [Heston et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.3535), [Gao et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2440866), [Baltussen et al., author-hosted published paper](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/intramom.pdf), [Livnat and Mendenhall](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1475-679X.2006.00196.x), [Feldman et al., author-hosted paper](https://bschool-en.huji.ac.il/sites/default/files/businesshe/files/managements_tone_change_post_earnings_announcement_drift_and_accruals.pdf). The proposed opening-range formula below is our hypothesis, not a claimed replication of these studies.

The existing multi-model work already covers daily momentum, reversal, volume continuation, compression, volatility, market-relative features and conditional combinations. Replacing a daily indicator name would not add this intraday clock or a new disclosure input. The completed next-open diagnostic likewise did not test true intraday signals. Reuse the source fixtures in [hourly access](../../docs/hourly-data-access.md) and [cost evidence](../../docs/trading-cost-evidence.md); do not repeat those probes as a new strategy study.

## Availability determines what can be traded

The documented free Alpaca plan excludes the newest 15 minutes of historical data; real-time IEX is a different feed. A 09:30–10:30 regular-session signal therefore cannot act at 10:30 using this access. The proposed earliest entry is **10:46 ET**, allowing the 15-minute restriction plus one minute for processing/order latency. Construct the opening hour from actual SIP minute bars; a provider 09:00–10:00 hourly bar contains premarket trading. Save revisions, missing minutes, mapping dates and partial sessions. Do not substitute IEX volume or fill missing observations with synthetic prices. [Alpaca access terms](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api), [aggregation and delayed-query rules](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq).

For filing drift, the earliest earnings press release may precede SEC acceptance. An accession-backed filing clock permits a **post-filing** test; it does not recreate the earlier earnings-announcement clock. A later 10-Q fact cannot become a feature at an earlier 8-K release. Preserve the original filing, its available timestamp, fiscal period, units and accession; use only filings already public at the decision time. SEC XBRL frames select recently filed facts for calendar periods and are not a ready-made historical information set. Quarterly/YTD durations, amendments, restatements, split-adjusted EPS and custom tags need explicit treatment. [SEC public API contract](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces).

A concrete future accounting signal could use `SUE = (EPS_q - EPS_q-4) / sd(previous eight known seasonal EPS differences)`, all on a comparable as-reported basis, with a five-session maximum hold. That is a separately testable seasonal-change proxy, not analyst surprise. Missing comparable facts mean unavailable signals, not reconstructed future values. A filing-text model would also need fixed dictionaries or historically appropriate training; a modern model's remembered future issuer outcomes must not contaminate a retrospective test. This memo does not authorize either implementation.

## One proposed experiment, fixed before outcomes

Reuse the **already declared six-name quote fixture**: AAPL, MSFT, F, T, HRL and BANF. This is a source/cost-feasibility pilot whose names were chosen independently of strategy returns, not the final trading universe or a change to the eleven-stock paper baseline. Proposed evaluation: 2017–2025, with 2016 reserved for warmup and source qualification. Full minute coverage remains to be verified; failures stay in the denominator with no replacement names or convenient date truncation. Only if this pilot is credible should a larger, predeclared sample selected by dated liquidity follow; it must not select the pilot's winning names or claim broad-stock coverage from six survivors.

For each actual exchange session, let `O,H,L,C,V` describe SIP minutes in `[09:30,10:30)`, and `r = log(C/O) - log(C_SPY/O_SPY)`. Use only a complete observed hour, `H>L`, and opening volume above its own previous 20-session opening-volume median. Rank within an arm by descending `abs(r)`, with alphabetical ties. The two long-only arms are:

- **Continuation:** `r>0` and `(C-L)/(H-L)>=0.75`.
- **Reversal:** `r<0` and `(C-L)/(H-L)<=0.25`.

Enter at the observed 10:46 minute open; exit at the observed 12:46 minute open, a fixed two-hour hold. These are reference-price simulations, not guaranteed fills. Both times lie within an ordinary or 13:00 early-close session. If the calendar or trading halt does not provide an exit, retain the unresolved required exit and invalidate that account path; never use future exit availability to remove an earlier signal. No intrabar stop/target optimization, overnight position, short sale, leverage or parameter sweep.

Keep USD2,000 cash, whole shares, at most five positions and a 20% entry cap. Plan the quantity using the known opening-hour close and a 0.5%-NAV budget divided by the **observed opening range**; this within-session raw-price distance avoids mixing split-adjusted daily ATR with raw minute units. Quantity may only decrease for fill-price affordability. This remains a simulated fill convention requiring separate execution qualification, and the range is a sizing proxy rather than a loss guarantee. Preserve settled cash and the historical settlement schedule; do not recycle unsettled sale proceeds intraday.

Declare **four cash paths: two arms × the existing proportional-40bps / Moomoo-plus-40bps scenarios**. Report all paths, including inactivity and failures. Add cash and the same-time unconditional eligible stock-event return as descriptive references; the latter is not a funded benchmark account. Report equal-date event means, actual net trade dollars, yearly/weekly stability, concentration, missing-data counts, settled-cash usage and the gap between signal-time and entry-time prices. Do not choose fees, thresholds or a winning arm from the outcomes. Reject the mechanism as useful here if it fails to improve the corresponding event reference, if gains disappear after the required delay/costs, or if profit depends on a few names/dates. A positive pilot would justify broader qualification only.

## Economic hurdle and why filings remain second

Under the **frozen Moomoo scenario**, a USD400, 40-share round trip has USD3.98 commission/platform minima; adding proportional 40bps contributes about USD1.60 at an unchanged price. That is about **1.40% of the ticket before regulatory/additional costs**. A USD200 ticket faces roughly 2.39%. These are scenario arithmetic, not the owner's confirmed promotion or a measured fill cost. Direct access to the linked tariff returned 403 in this review; the preserved indexed tariff evidence remains in the cost document. The proportional-only scenario is also a sensitivity, not proof of a commission-free executable route. [Provider tariff](https://www.moomoo.com/ca/support/topic10_122).

That hurdle makes frequent small tickets difficult. The modern US T+1 cycle does not turn settled cash into unlimited intraday buying power; earlier research dates require their earlier settlement rules. [SEC settlement rule](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2023/34-96930.pdf). Neither shortening holds nor avoiding overnight exposure creates an economic edge.

Filing strategies may offer fewer, larger informational events over several sessions, but no-consensus data and the five-session ceiling weaken direct transfer from classic PEAD evidence. Transaction costs are particularly relevant: Ng, Rusticus and Verdi find that costs substantially reduce implementable PEAD profits and that larger apparent drifts occur in more costly firms. [Original paper](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1475-679X.2008.00290.x). The hourly pilot therefore has the best immediate **information value per bounded research effort**, while the filing lane remains the stronger candidate for a genuinely new fundamental input after its event history is qualified. Neither is a recommendation to trade or a reason to discard the completed daily evidence.
