# Delayed opening continuation versus reversal

Pre-execution design, 2026-09-28. This is the four-path source/cost-feasibility pilot proposed in `conditional-selection20260928/next-alpha-research.md`, whose original text remains unchanged. No actual strategy outcome has been inspected for this implementation. AAPL, MSFT, F, T, HRL and BANF are the six previously declared quote-fixture stocks; SPY is a reference only. Evaluate every exchange session in 2017–2025, with 2016 warmup, retaining every unavailable observation and failure. These surviving names do not represent the final broad universe.

One formula refinement is declared before outcomes: replace opening **share volume** with opening **dollar activity**, `sum(minute_close * minute_volume)`. A split changes raw share units across sessions; the dollar proxy makes the comparison less dependent on that unit change. It is a close-price turnover proxy, not exact traded dollar volume, and does not make revised data historically point-in-time. No other threshold or hypothesis is added.

## Causal features and four paths

Use the 60 exact raw SIP minute bars starting at each actual exchange open, `[09:30,10:30)` America/New_York. Require all 60 valid distinct stock and SPY minutes. Aggregate first open O, maximum H, minimum L, last close C and dollar activity A. Require H>L. Require all of the stock's immediately preceding 20 exchange-session opening hours to be complete, without skipping missing dates, and `A > median(A[t-20:t])`. Past activity does not require a past SPY hour because it is a stock-only quantity; current relative return does require SPY.

Compute `r = log(C/O) - log(C_SPY/O_SPY)` and `location = (C-L)/(H-L)`. Continuation admits `r>0` and `location>=0.75`; reversal admits `r<0` and `location<=0.25`. Rank by decreasing abs(r), then alphabetically. This ranking contains no future execution or outcome availability. Signals finish at 10:30, become usable after the declared 15-minute SIP restriction and one-minute processing allowance, and enter at the observed **10:46 minute open**. Exit at the observed **12:46 minute open**. Both times precede the 13:00 early close. No short, overnight position, stop/target or parameter sweep is introduced.

Exactly four continuous accounts: continuation and reversal, each with the existing `proportional` and `moomoo_ca_us` profiles at 40-bps round-trip friction. Begin with USD2,000. Reuse `frontier.execution_costs.get_cost_profile` and `frontier.historical_settlement.build_settlement_indices` unchanged, preserving the declared current-tariff counterfactual and historical bank/exchange intersection T+3/T+2/T+1 availability. No unsettled proceeds are spent, including intraday proceeds. No risk stop is added to the research accounts.

## Planning, fills and unresolved prices

At signal time, calculate account NAV from cash including sale receivables; every valid prior session has already exited. In ranking order reserve at most five orders. Each planned whole quantity is at most `floor(0.005 * NAV / (H-L))`; its notional plus buy fee must fit both 20% of that NAV and remaining unreserved settled cash, using the known opening-hour C. Skip zero quantities and continue down the original ranking. Reservations therefore include fees. At entry, each order has its own fixed cash reservation; reduce quantity only as needed to fit that reservation and remaining settled cash at the observed fill price. Never increase the planned quantity or replace an order using future prices. A price gap can cancel an otherwise valid planned order if no share is affordable. Record every planned quantity, reservation, actual quantity and signal-to-entry price gap.

A missing required entry price for a planned positive-quantity order invalidates the account at that event; it is not a skipped candidate. A missing required exit for an actual position likewise preserves the unresolved position and invalidates the account. No future exit-availability filter screens a signal. Record the failure and all completed/pending activity; suppress full-period economic success metrics. Invalid/missing source bars remain explicit through the adapter's source evidence. Opening or warmup missingness causes causal abstention; post-entry sparse bars do not manufacture a fill or bar.

## Intraday NAV and references

Mark held positions at every observed held-symbol minute-close boundary during the two-hour hold, using each symbol's most recently observed close. At a boundary where another held symbol has no bar, carry its last observed valuation price with explicit age; this is a stale valuation, not an invented minute bar. Include separate entry/exit open-price and post-fee marks at their exact UTC timestamps. Preserve marked NAV, settled/unsettled cash, marked positions value, stale-position count and maximum mark age. Report **sampled-mark drawdown**, not an intrabar maximum-loss guarantee. No daily-only curve can stand in for these observations. Return summaries also retain per-session closing cash/NAV, weekly/monthly/yearly returns, fees, exposure and trade concentration inputs. Period returns chain from the preceding period's closing equity, without resetting account capital. Result metadata explicitly identifies raw same-session entry/exit notionals; the unchanged shared cost specification's older synthetic-adjusted-exit caveat does not describe this pilot's price convention.

The descriptive unconditional event reference uses the same complete-hour, 20-session warmup, activity and nonzero-range eligibility, omitting only the r sign and location tests. For each eligible stock-session calculate the observed 12:46-open / 10:46-open gross return when both endpoints exist, after eligibility is fixed. Preserve separate missing-entry, missing-exit and matched denominators without reranking or replacing names. First average matched event returns across stocks within each date, then average those date means; dates with no matched reference are unavailable, not zero. This is an unfunded event diagnostic, not an executable benchmark or portfolio return. Cash is the funded zero-return reference.

Root's capture adapter verifies raw/source identities, excludes duplicate/invalid bars only from this canonical lookup while preserving them in coverage evidence, and retains empty/error cells. No network call or source mutation belongs in the strategy module. Four calls reuse identical source inputs. The runner must retain source failures and account failures rather than calling a complete transport a complete strategy dataset.

Freeze request, implementation and source identities before actual evaluation. Report all four accounts and missing denominators, the event reference, fees, sampled drawdown, weekly/monthly/yearly consistency, stock/trade concentration and delayed execution gaps.
