## Components and interfaces
1. execution_costs.py: immutable ExecutionCosts profile and factory get_cost_profile(name,round_trip_bps=40). Explicit profiles proportional, wealthsimple_usd, moomoo_ca_us. fee(quantity,notional,side)->float is a monetary cost including declared proportional market-friction scenario; maximum_quantity(max_quantity,price,budget,side='buy')->int respects fixed/minimum charges. Frozen serializable specification includes tariff date/source URLs, assumptions and excluded charges. Default proportional model exactly preserves legacy arithmetic. Broker schedules are current-tariff counterfactuals when applied to older history; market friction is a declared sensitivity, not calibrated realized slippage. Rounding and unknown fee components must be explicit. Lower-bound schedules must be labeled and may not establish net profitability.
2. short_horizon_study._simulate_daily adds cost_model=None and process_through=None. end remains fixed last session of the full rehearsal. process_through limits the current chunk; returned state carries positions, pending orders, receivables and next_session_index, with policy/market-layout cursor consistency checks; the historical replay caller independently verifies immutable market and signal content through source/model/code identities. Resume at that exact next index; defaults retain all prior metrics/trade timing. Positions must not reset between chunks. Per-chunk curves/trades/fees/activity compose to a monolithic run exactly. The planner accepts cost_model=None and reserves actual modeled entry costs, preserving default behavior.
