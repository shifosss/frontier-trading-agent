### What these costs mean at the illustrative scale

These are arithmetic examples, not return forecasts:

- With flat security prices and flat base exchange rates, two 1.5% Wealthsimple CAD-account conversions retain `0.985 × 0.985 = 97.0225%`: approximately **CAD20.84 of CAD700** disappears before corporate-rate spreads or market spreads.
- CAD10/month equals **1.43% of a CAD700 monthly addition**, before tax. That is a contribution comparison, not an annual portfolio return calculation.
- A CAD140 stock position at moomoo incurs at least **CAD2.98 round trip**, or **2.13%**, before spread/other charges. A US$100 stock ticket has at least **US$3.98 round-trip** commission/platform cost.

## Options scope under the cash-account mandate

Wealthsimple permits approved eligible accounts to buy and sell long calls/puts. Its published covered-call eligibility lists registered accounts and **non-registered margin accounts**, not the user's proposed taxable cash account. Therefore the project must not assume that covered calls or secured-put writing are available in that account. No margin-account opening or upgrade is implied. [Options eligibility](https://help.wealthsimple.com/hc/en-ca/articles/9606205720987-Enable-options-trading-in-your-investing-account), [covered-call account restrictions](https://help.wealthsimple.com/hc/en-ca/articles/39256656417819-Sell-a-covered-call).

Moomoo Canada's options pages include cash accounts; its Canada options FAQ prohibits naked short options in cash accounts. That does not establish this user's approved strategy level or permission for a particular spread/writing strategy. US-account option-level tables must not be substituted for Canadian permissions. [Canada options/account support](https://www.moomoo.com/ca/invest/options), [Canada options FAQ](https://www.moomoo.com/ca/fr/support/topic10_142).

A standard equity option commonly represents 100 shares, so a US$1 premium commonly costs US$100 for one contract; adjusted contracts can differ. A long option can expire worthless. An in-the-money long option can also become a stock purchase or sale at exercise, requiring cash or shares beyond the premium. Wealthsimple attempts automatic exercise at expiry when an option is at least US$0.01 in the money. Fully paying the premium therefore does not settle the expiry-funding problem. [Contract units](https://www.moomoo.com/ca/fr/support/topic10_142), [Wealthsimple exercise handling](https://help.wealthsimple.com/hc/en-ca/articles/39056801816859-Understand-exercising-options-and-assignment).

**Current operating decision:** keep options as research until there is a dated chain/quote source, contract multiplier and expiry handling, verified cash-account permissions, explicit cash maximum loss, and a tested close-before-expiry process. A stock momentum score alone does not justify an option strike or expiry. Monthly funding and a 30% drawdown tolerance create no obligation to trade; a zero-quantity or cash result is valid. The stock scanner's ATR sizing is a risk-distance estimate, not a guaranteed loss ceiling. Actual equity and cash flows are needed before a 30% drawdown control can be measured correctly.

## Taxable records the system needs

- **CAD accounting for each transaction:** translate sale proceeds using the rate at sale, acquisition cost using the rate at acquisition, and fees at their respective dates. Converting the final USD trading profit at one exchange rate is insufficient. [CRA capital-gain calculation](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/personal-income/line-12700-capital-gains/calculating-reporting-your-capital-gains-losses.html).
- **Average adjusted cost base (ACB):** identical shares use average cost, including acquisition expenses, with corporate-action adjustments. Operational implication: combine the owner's relevant taxable records from both brokers before calculating ACB; each broker sees only its own ledger. T5008 box 20 may not equal the investor's correct ACB. [CRA identical-property rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4037/capital-gains.html), [CRA T5008 guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/completing-slips-summaries/financial-slips-summaries/return-securities-transactions-t5008/t5008-slip.html).
- **Loss/re-entry tracking:** for capital treatment, the superficial-loss rule can deny a loss when identical property is acquired in the 30 days before/after a loss sale and is still owned 30 days afterward; affiliated-person activity can matter. Rebuy signals must preserve these records rather than assuming each realized loss is immediately deductible. [CRA capital losses](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/personal-income/line-12700-capital-gains/capital-losses-deductions.html).
- **USD cash and classification:** foreign-currency cash can have its own capital gains/losses; CRA's CAD200 annual exclusion concerns qualifying foreign-currency money gains, not a blanket exclusion for US-stock profits. Frequent, short-duration stock/options trading may instead be business income depending on the facts; “taxable account” does not resolve that classification. Retain option premiums, sales, expiries, exercises/assignments, linked stock transactions, cash conversions, dividends and withholding separately. [CRA foreign currency rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/personal-income/line-12700-capital-gains/completing-schedule-3.html), [CRA securities business/capital factors](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/technical-information/income-tax-audit-manual-domestic-compliance-programs-branch-dcpb-27.html).

Broker export reconciliation, current spendable CAD/USD balances, account-specific permissions and a prospective net-of-cost paper record remain necessary for account-ready execution.
