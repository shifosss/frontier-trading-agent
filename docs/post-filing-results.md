# Original-filing strategy results

**September 28, 2026: all four declared accounts and independent audits completed, with no invalid account. The income filter improves the account comparison, but does not establish a useful trading edge.** Its best scenario gains only USD2.53 on USD2,000 over the 2017–2025 event period. The broker-fee scenario loses USD73.23. No strategy is promoted.

## Fixed comparison

The [source and feature study](post-filing-research.md) uses original quarterly net-income changes divided by prior fiscal-year assets, standardized against the issuer's previous eight original observations. The treatment admits scores of at least one; the control admits every structurally eligible filing. Both decide after conservative disclosure availability, enter at the following eligible close, hold five sessions, use whole shares and settled cash, and retain the original fee-inclusive reservation.

The six predeclared issuers yield 121 eligible filings in 2017–2025, of which 27 pass the income threshold. Ford has no eligible evaluation event because missing required original concepts interrupt its history. All missing filings and facts remain represented. Twenty-seven selected events across five issuers and nine years fall below the declared 30-event minimum; no threshold or issuer was changed to pass it.

The proportional scenario charges 40 basis points round trip. The Moomoo scenario adds the previously frozen Canadian-account US-equity tariff. These are sensitivity/counterfactual assumptions, not verified realized fills or complete historical broker charges. USD funding, FX, taxes and account charges remain excluded.

## Why the improvement is not a qualified edge

The proportional income account's average capital exposure is only **0.325%**, versus 1.602% for the unconditional control. It makes 23 plans and 19 fills from 27 signals; whole-share sizing and the original reservation prevent some entries. It gains an average USD0.133 per completed trade, while the equally weighted mean trade return is negative, −0.152%. Different position sizes explain that distinction. Its largest winning trade, USD4.47, exceeds the entire USD2.53 account gain.

Of the USD60.22 improvement over the proportional control, USD45.18 is lower modeled cost and USD15.03 is higher gross trading dollars. The Moomoo improvement of USD288.62 consists of USD275.18 lower costs and USD13.44 higher gross dollars. This is an arithmetic decomposition of the different recorded account paths, not a causal estimate holding sizes and trades fixed. Most relative improvement comes from paying fewer fees.

The proportional income account has four positive years, two negative years and three years without a trade. The Moomoo income account has no profitable year. Low drawdown here mostly accompanies very low investment; it does not demonstrate a dependable 30% loss ceiling.

Weekly and monthly account returns, including inactive periods, are preserved. The proportional income account has positive returns in 2.75% of 473 weekly periods and 7.27% of 110 monthly periods; most periods are flat. The Moomoo equivalents are 0.85% and 1.82%. First/last periods can be partial. Unchanged cash earns zero in this comparison; interest and opportunity cost are not modeled.

The separate, unfunded event reference is less encouraging for stock-specific selection. All 121 eligible and 27 selected events have matched stock/SPY endpoints. Selected stocks average +0.766% gross over five sessions, but simultaneous SPY returns average +0.722%, leaving only **4.36 basis points of mean excess return**. The unconditional events average 22.86 basis points of excess return. Giving each entry date equal weight produces 1.86 versus 21.10 basis points, respectively. These overlapping survivor-sample observations are descriptive, without costs, cash constraints or significance claims. The account improvement therefore does not establish that the income filter selects stronger market-relative opportunities.
