## The proposed comparison

The [full research memo](../.qualification/filing-feasibility20260928/strategy-memo.md) proposes four USD2,000 accounts: an unusually positive seasonal-income signal versus an unconditional filing-event control, both using a fixed five-session hold under the two existing cost scenarios. This is a post-filing hypothesis; it is not analyst-consensus earnings surprise or an assumption that classic earnings-drift findings prove a one-week edge.

For each original 10-Q, obtain the current-quarter and comparable prior-year-quarter `us-gaap:NetIncomeLoss`, plus prior fiscal year-end `us-gaap:Assets`, from that same accession. Divide the income change by those assets. Standardize using only the issuer's previous eight qualified, already disclosed innovations; admit a signal at one historical standard deviation or above. Prior observations remain as originally reported, without later restatement backfills. Missing or ambiguous contexts interrupt the required history. There is no EPS split repair, Q4 derived from a later annual report, threshold sweep or substitute issuer.

Use the later of the verified acceptance date in New York and official filing date, with overnight processing, then a following exchange-session close. A later 10-Q fact cannot be assigned to an earlier earnings-release timestamp. Whole shares, settled cash, next-executable timing, explicit costs and all failed/abstaining paths remain required. The original six pilot names are a source-feasibility sample; a broad universe still needs separately declared dated eligibility.
